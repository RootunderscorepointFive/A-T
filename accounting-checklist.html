Is your accounting
ready to support growth?

Work through the checklist honestly. The gaps will show you where financial administration is creating risk, delay, or uncertainty.

How to use it

Mark each item that is consistently true. If fewer than three items are true in any section, that area deserves attention. You can print this page or save it as a PDF from your browser.

Monthly bookkeeping

All business transactions are recorded and categorised each month.
Every bank and credit account is reconciled.
Personal and business expenses are kept separate.
Receivables and payables are reviewed regularly.

Tax and payroll

VAT records reflect the correct treatment for each transaction.
SARS submissions are supported by complete records.
Payroll records agree to payments and submissions.
Upcoming filing dates are tracked in advance.

Reporting and decisions

Management receives current financial reports.
Cash flow is reviewed before commitments are made.
Actual performance is compared with a budget or forecast.
Leadership understands what is driving profit and cash movement.

Systems and controls

Supporting documents can be found quickly.
Access to financial systems is controlled.
Accounting responsibilities are clearly assigned.
Data can be exported and backed up when needed.
